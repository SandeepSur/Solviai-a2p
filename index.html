Why great roofing work still goes unnoticed

Most roofing companies deliver excellent work but rely on memory, busy staff, and inconsistent follow-ups to collect reviews. The result is missed opportunities and competitors appearing more trusted online.

Reviews Are Never Requested

The roof is completed, the homeowner is satisfied, and everyone moves on without asking for a review.

Follow-Ups Depend on Staff

Your team has to remember which homeowners to contact, when to message them, and whether they responded.

Past Roofing Customers Stay Untapped

Years of completed roof replacements and repairs sit inside spreadsheets and CRMs without generating new reviews.

Customer Concerns Surface Too Late

Problems are sometimes discovered only after the homeowner posts publicly or stops responding to your team.
